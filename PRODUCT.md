# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two overlapping segments: early-stage researchers (Sydney homeowners imagining their future build, comparing options) and late-stage buyers (committed to building, comparing builders on trust and delivery). Both arrive via search or referral. The early-stage visitor needs to be inspired and educated; the late-stage buyer needs to trust and contact. The site must serve both without patronising either.

## Product Purpose

Convert visitors into consultation enquiries. The site is the primary sales tool for a boutique Sydney residential builder founded in 2016. It must communicate craft, trustworthiness, and clarity — and feel nothing like a volume builder's catalogue.

## Brand Personality

Modern · Considered · Confident. Voice is direct without being brusque; warm without being folksy; premium without being unattainable. The brand sells precision: fixed price, no surprises, since 2016.

## Anti-references

- Generic volume builders (Metricon, Simonds): cheap stock-photo heroes, numbered package cards, cluttered promo banners.
- Generic AI / agency templates: centred-everything layouts, 3-feature card grids, artificial testimonial carousels, eyebrows on every section.

## Design Principles

1. Tell a story, not a brochure. Every section advances a narrative from "imagine your home" to "let's build it."
2. Scroll is the medium. Motion is earned — it reveals, it paces, it does not decorate.
3. Honest craft. No invented metrics. No fabricated superlatives. The work speaks first.
4. Asymmetry as intention. Equal-width grids and centred layouts are the default; breaking them signals deliberate choice.
5. Restraint at the macro, richness at the micro. Clean section structure with intricate details — transitions, type, material texture — that reward attention.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. prefers-reduced-motion respected across all animations. All body text ≥ 4.5:1 contrast against background. Focus states always visible.
